Why NeedSoon

Forgotten buys start with forgotten adds.

The problem doesn’t happen at the store. It happens at home.

You open the fridge: “We’re almost out of milk.” You reach for the refill: “Did we run out?” If you don’t note it right then, it’s gone by the time you reach the store.

Most shopping list apps are built for the shopping trip. NeedSoon is built for the second you notice⁠—and we cut every step we could between that moment and a saved item.

Help & support

Using NeedSoon

There are just two screens: Add and List. Switch with the tabs at the bottom, or swipe from the screen edge.

Adding items

Type an item name in the field on the Add screen⁠—the first thing you see when you open the app.

  • Re-add recent and frequent items with one tap.
  • After adding, you’ll see “Added ‘Milk’” with an Undo button.
  • If the same item is already on your list, NeedSoon tells you instead of adding a duplicate.
  • Tap an item to edit its name, quantity, notes, or the store you plan to buy it at.
Adding by voice, barcode, or label photo
  • Voice: say several items at once and they’re split up (“milk and eggs” → 2 items). Deselect any you don’t want on the confirmation screen.
  • Scan: scan a barcode to fill in the product name. If nothing is found, switch to typing.
  • Capture: photograph a product label and text is recognized on your device as suggestions (no connection needed).

Camera and microphone permission is requested only the first time you use those features. Every core feature works by typing if you decline.

Adding from widgets, Siri, or Apple Watch
  • Widgets: touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen and add NeedSoon. Start with the voice, scan, or camera buttons.
  • Control Center: add the control to open the add screen in one tap.
  • Siri: say “Add to NeedSoon” and answer the follow-up question.
  • Apple Watch: installs automatically on your paired Watch. Add by voice or text, and check items off from your list.
Sharing with family, and leaving
  1. On the List screen, tap the family icon at the top left.
  2. Create a family to show a QR code.
  3. On the other device, open the same screen and choose Scan QR to join.

Syncing happens when you open the app, pull to refresh, or make a change. The family name and the auto-delete period for purchased items are shared by everyone.

To stop sharing, choose “Leave this family.” That device returns to local-only use. Only show your QR code to people you trust.

While shopping
  • Sort: by date added, by aisle, by store, or by person.
  • Check off: one tap when you buy it⁠—tap again to undo.
  • Stores: set a store for each item, or for many items at once.
Settings (auto-delete, character)
  • Auto-delete purchased items: after 7–90 days, or never (default 90 days; shared across the family while sharing).
  • Character: set your icon (an animal avatar or a photo) and name, shown as “who added” when sharing.
  • Open Settings from the menu at the top right of the List screen.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account to share with my family or partner?

No. There’s no sign-up and no email address. Create a family to show a QR code; anyone who scans it shares the same shopping list. Behind the scenes, devices that know the same random family ID (the QR code) sync with each other.

Is NeedSoon free?

Yes. Download it free from the App Store and start right away.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Adding, editing, and checking off items all work offline. Family sharing syncs when you’re back online or open the app.

Do I need a separate Apple Watch app?

No. It’s included with the iPhone app and installs on your paired Apple Watch automatically. View your list, add by voice or text, and check items off.

A scanned barcode isn’t found

Type the item name and add it as usual. Store-specific codes that can’t be looked up show “Product not found” and switch to manual entry. Items without a barcode can also be added by label photo or voice.

Sharing doesn’t work on my work-managed iPhone

Syncing may fail on networks with restrictions. You can still add and check off items on the device. If it doesn’t improve, please contact us.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items in the app. To stop sharing, choose “Leave this family.” Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To delete shared data, contact us below.
